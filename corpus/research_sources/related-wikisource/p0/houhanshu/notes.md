# 《後漢書》 accession notes

- 卷83 is 嚴光 in 〈逸民列傳〉 and reads “客星犯御坐甚急”.
- 卷100〈天文上〉 repeats the story in a 古今注 note as “客星犯帝坐甚急”.
- The two pages belong to the same received-history/Wikisource family; they expose a material wording/location difference but are not independent witnesses.
- Both are mapped to C45-H2 as comparison evidence only; neither silently corrects the carrier's “天子宿／嚴遵” wording.
