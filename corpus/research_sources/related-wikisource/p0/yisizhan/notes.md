# 《乙巳占》 accession notes

- Fixed pages: 卷二、卷五、卷八. 卷二 is retained as a complete page object but is not Core14 C13 evidence.
- C13 is the 卷五 reading “火逆行氐，失地，一曰多火災”; “一曰” is an internal alternative in this same transcription, not an independent witness.
- The same 卷五 page also supplies the C09 near formula “土木合，其邦饑，戰不勝，亡地”.
- C47 is in 卷八〈彗孛入列宿占第四十八〉: “大臣誅” and the 30/50/70-day sequence. Preserve these beside, rather than overwrite, the 《唐開元占經》 readings “謀” and “時”.
- Wikisource gives one transcription family and no verified print-edition siglum; agreement with a mirror is not independent attestation.
