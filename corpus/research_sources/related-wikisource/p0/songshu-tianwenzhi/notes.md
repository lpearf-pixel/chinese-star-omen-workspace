# 《宋書·天文志》 accession notes

- The complete separable Wikisource astronomy-treatise run is captured as 卷23–26 (天文一–天文四).
- C09 direct evidence is in 卷25: “歲星犯填星，在參…歲、填合為內亂…益州戰不勝，亡地”. The lunar–Jupiter notices in 卷24 are not C09.
- C43 direct evidence is in 卷23: “客星見危，逆行在離宮北，騰蛇南”.
- C14 material preserves the explicit reservation “宜是入太微” against “熒惑守心”; both readings remain visible.
- 卷25 also preserves the C11 historical parallel “太白奄熒惑，在營室…金火合為爍”.
- 沈約 is recorded as historical compiler; the fixed raw volume headers leave the author field blank.
