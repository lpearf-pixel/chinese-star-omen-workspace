# 《史記·天官書》 accession notes

- The fixed root object records the Wikisource work-level version metadata; the fixed 卷027 page preserves 〈天官書第五〉 and its integrated commentary markup.
- C03 directly maps to “月蝕歳星，其宿地，飢若亡”. The adjoining 正義 explains “月蝕星” as the moon covering the star, supporting the occultation reading rather than an ordinary lunar eclipse.
- The root and 卷027 are source objects in one Wikisource presentation, not independent witnesses.
