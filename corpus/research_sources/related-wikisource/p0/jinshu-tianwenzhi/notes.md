# 《晉書·天文志》 accession notes

- The complete separable Wikisource astronomy-treatise run is captured as 卷011–013 (天文上、中、下).
- 卷012 supplies C03 lunar–Jupiter notices and the C11 parallel “太白奄熒惑，在營室…金火合為爍，為兵喪”.
- 卷013 supplies C14 historical material including the “疑是入太微” reservation.
- C47 links are contextual/terminological: 卷012 defines 彗/掃, while 卷013 records broom/comet paths. These do not silently replace the East-Well rules in the carrier.
- All three pages are one Wikisource transcription family.
